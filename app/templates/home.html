Donating Materials

Please get in touch if you would like to donate material to the library. It's helpful for us to know in advance what you are planning to donate, because not everything is suitable for our collection. You can get in touch with us through library@coventry.edu.kz.

✓ As a general guide we would welcome as donations:

  • Extra copies of current books in demand
  • Recently published materials that fit our teaching or research profile
  • Rare items that are suitable for special collections

✗ Unfortunately, we are not able to take:

  • Old superseded editions of textbooks
  • Duplicates of existing holdings where we don't need any further copies
  • Journals/periodicals
  • Items in poor condition
  • Large collections that we can't accommodate
  • Items that don't fit our teaching/research profile

Accepted donations will be evaluated by a subject specialist, and we reserve the right to dispose of any items we feel unsuitable for the collection.

Referencing & Citation

Referencing in APA Style (7th Edition)

ℹ️ Introduction

There are two parts to APA referencing: in-text citations and a list of references.

  • In-text citations: Short references in the body of your text.
  • List of References: Full details of every source at the end of your work.

1. In-text Citations

An in-text citation must be included whenever you use information from elsewhere (quote, paraphrase, summary, data, etc.). It normally contains:

  • Author's last name
  • Year of publication
  • Page number(s) (if quoting)
Parenthetical Citation

The study showed a significant increase (Smith, 2021, p. 25).

Narrative Citation

Smith (2021) noted that "results were significant" (p. 25).

2. List of References

The list goes at the end of your assignment and gives full details of sources. Use a hanging indent for each entry.

Example Entry

Gemen, H. (2015). Agricultural finance: From crops to land, water and infrastructure. John Wiley. https://doi.org/10.1002/9781118827352

Format: Alphabetical order by author's last name. Use the Reference List generator in Word or Zotero/Mendeley to automate this.

Citation Tools

Bibliographic management tools interface directly with library databases and allow you to collect, organize, and cite your references automatically. The library supports the following tools:

Zotero

Recommended

Free "bibliographic citation management" software that works inside your browser (Firefox/Chrome) to save citations and organize research.

In a Nutshell:
  1. Install: Zotero Standalone or Browser Extension.
  2. Plugin: Install the word-processor plugin (Word/Open Office).
  3. Configure: Set your citation style (e.g., APA).
  4. Usage: Automatically grab PDFs and sync data across computers.
